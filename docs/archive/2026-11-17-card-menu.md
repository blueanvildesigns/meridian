# Decision: card more-options menu

Date: 2026-11-17
Source: design interview, round 7. Mockups: canvas "Meridian Web: Design Canvas", row "Card menu: rename, reorder, remove" (`CardMenu`, `CardMenuRename`, `CardMenuRemoved`, `CardMenuPhone`).

## Decisions

1. **Menu items:** Jump from here, Rename, Move up, Move down, then a separator and Remove (destructive, in red). The drag handle stays for mouse and touch reordering; Move up and Move down give keyboard and screen-reader users an equivalent.
2. **Presentation (proposed by Claude, consistent with the other overlays):** a small popover menu anchored to the card's more-options button on desktop, a bottom sheet on phones. The phone sheet shows the city name and current time at its top and ends with a Cancel button.
3. **Rename: edit in place on the card.** The name becomes a text field, with Save and Cancel icon buttons for touch users, and the hint "Enter to save · Esc to cancel". Only the display name changes; the stored zone stays the same.
4. **Remove: immediate, with an Undo toast.** No confirmation dialog. A toast at the bottom center reads "<City> removed" with an Undo button.
5. **Semantics:** the menu uses `role="menu"` and `role="menuitem"`; the more-options button uses `aria-haspopup` and `aria-expanded`. Targets are at least 44 px. Focus starts on the first item.

## Proposed by Claude, not yet confirmed

- Move up is disabled on the first card and Move down on the last card.
- "My Local Time" gets the same menu. It can be renamed and removed, and it can be restored from the Add city dialog (already specified).
- The Undo toast stays for a few seconds (suggest about 8 s), is announced to screen readers as a status message, and Undo restores the city to its previous position. Removing a second city before the first toast expires replaces the toast.

## Open points

- Exact toast duration, and whether Undo is also reachable by keyboard shortcut.
- Whether renaming should be validated (empty name, maximum length). Suggest: an empty name reverts to the city's original name.
- Not yet designed: settings, About page, empty list state, error states, app-wide dark mode.
